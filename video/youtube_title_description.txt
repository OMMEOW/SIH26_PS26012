TITLE
GeoParcel AI: Drone Imagery → Land-Record Parcel Maps with AI | SIH 2026 PS 26012

DESCRIPTION
GeoParcel AI — our Smart India Hackathon 2026 solution for Problem Statement 26012 (Ministry of Rural Development, Department of Land Resources): AI-based automated urban parcel mapping and cadastral feature extraction from drone imagery.

Drone surveys under NAKSHA and SVAMITVA capture imagery fast, but turning it into land-record maps still means tracing every building by hand. GeoParcel AI segments building footprints with a U-Net (ResNet-34 encoder), converts them to polygons, regularises them into clean right-angled shapes, and exports georeferenced GeoJSON parcel candidates for Web-GIS review and field verification.

Results (Open Cities AI Challenge drone imagery, 7.2 cm/pixel, trained on one laptop):
• Pixel IoU 0.60 on 502 held-out tiles
• Regularisation: ~27× fewer vertices per polygon, right-angle corners 17% → 35%, polygon IoU 0.47 → 0.46
• Satellite-resolution imagery (multi-scale model, unseen scene resampled to 45 cm/pixel): pixel IoU 0.14 → 0.61; at drone resolution 0.61 → 0.67
• Unseen scene (≈396 m × 396 m neighbourhood at 4.8 cm/pixel, never used in training): pixel IoU 0.80, 427 parcel candidates exported as GeoJSON

Scope: outputs are preliminary parcel candidates for field verification — decision support, not an automated legal judgement. Known limitations: touching buildings with shared walls can merge into one candidate; oblique high-rise towers are often missed (no such buildings in the training data).

Code and model weights: https://github.com/OMMEOW/SIH26_PS26012
